DailyCollabs local development

Keep the dailycollabs-mongo Docker container running, then run this from the project folder:

  node scripts/dev.cjs

App:             http://localhost:5173/
Communities:     http://localhost:5173/Communities
Profile:         http://localhost:5173/Settings
My comments:     http://localhost:5173/Comments
Notifications:   http://localhost:5173/Notifications
Welcome:         http://localhost:5173/Welcome
Mission control: http://localhost:5173/Agents
API:             http://localhost:4000/
AI health:       http://localhost:5000/health

The launcher starts the API, frontend, AI service (using ai_service/.venv), and
quality watcher together. Ctrl+C stops them together. Existing .env files stay
local. The frontend uses polling to detect saved changes reliably.

The quality watcher checks service health every 15 seconds and runs a fresh
frontend production build and feature tests after saved source changes. It
records actual outcomes in Mission control. Coding agents investigate and
correct during an active chat; they are available for the next mission when
their reviewed work is complete. Give new missions in the Codex chat.

Each specialist's research ledger is in research/agent-1.md through agent-4.md.
All four reviewed at least 15 different website domains. Source links are also
available on each Mission control card.

Tests (from the project folder):
  node --test Backend/tests/*.test.cjs

Frontend build (from Frontend/client):
  npm run build

Five demo boards initialize once after MongoDB connects. Their opening prompts
are labeled as sample conversations; memberships and new posts are real data.
Invitation links lead back to their community after login or registration.

Video links support YouTube, Vimeo, and direct HTTPS MP4/WebM. Provider embeds
and native video sources start muted when visible in For you, with Unmute/Mute
controls and offscreen pause. Other views load them when selected. Optional transcript text is saved
with shared videos. A source owner may still restrict embedding or captions.

The topic search prompt stays pinned while browsing. Missing or broken images
receive stable, distinct artwork per card. See research/algorithm/README.md for
the 89-site research ledger and README.md for feed preference controls.
